# Roadmap

1. Foundation: Laravel, Docker, PostgreSQL, Redis, CI, `.env.example`, ADRs, health checks.
2. Identity/farm/pond: authentication, roles, policies, farm isolation, pond status.
3. Cultivation/monitoring: historical cycles, growth, water quality, health, mortality.
4. Feeding/reminders: stock consistency, queued notifications, idempotent jobs.
5. Prediction/harvest: deterministic prediction with assumptions and uncertainty.
6. AI: backend adapter, fake provider, validation, provenance, limits, graceful failure.
7. Simulator/reporting: protected simulator, ingestion idempotency, authorized exports.
8. IoT readiness: authenticated ingestion contract, deduplication, replay protection.
